# Third-party assets

## Kenney Tower Defense assets

Tower sprites used by the Phaser v8 battle client are from Kenney's Tower Defense asset pack, accessed through the public `jhonnold/tower-defense-v2` GitHub repository.

License: CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain.

Source repository: https://github.com/jhonnold/tower-defense-v2

Files referenced by the game include Kenney tower tiles 180, 181, 205, 206, and 249 from the Retina PNG set.

These assets are used as an interim production-art foundation and may be repainted or replaced by bespoke Ottoman assets later.
