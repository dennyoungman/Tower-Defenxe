# Chronicle Defence — Campaign Foundation

## Core identity
Chronicle Defence is a grand-strategy tower-defence game. The persistent campaign is the game; tower defence is the tactical battle engine.

Core loop:
World Map → Strategic Turn → Decision/Conflict → TD Battle → Persistent Consequences → World Map.

## Persistent regional economy
Every region tracks population, prosperity, production, food, trade, stability, fortification, infrastructure, strategic resource, ownership, occupation and battle damage.

Regional output is contextual rather than fixed. Hostile borders and wars reduce output. Friendly borders improve it. Infrastructure and stability multiply output. Occupation and battle damage suppress output.

## Global economy
Primary campaign pools: Treasury, Food/Supply, Production, Knowledge and Manpower. Strategic resources remain properties of regions/trade rather than becoming many separate currencies.

War has upkeep. Frontier regions bordering active enemies add military costs. Empire size eventually reduces administrative efficiency unless technology/state systems offset it.

## Battle ↔ campaign coupling
A region's prosperity, infrastructure, frontier situation, fortification and global treasury determine starting resources for its TD battle.

TD results return persistent consequences: base health, breaches, casualties, spending and victory/defeat can damage infrastructure/economy, reduce stability/manpower, cause occupation or transfer ownership.

A technical victory can therefore still leave a region devastated.

## Diplomacy
Friendly borders reduce economic friction and can improve prosperity. Alliances later permit military access, auxiliaries and intelligence. Hostile borders cause disruption and garrison pressure. Active war produces severe frontier and supply penalties.

## Expansion
Conquered regions begin occupied with reduced stability/economic efficiency and require time/investment to become productive. Expansion is therefore not automatically profitable.

## Strategic turns
Global time advances in strategic turns. Every turn processes regional output, war/frontier upkeep, recovery, stability, objectives and eventually AI actions/events.

## Objectives and victory
Objectives can require state over time, not just one-time achievements. Examples:
- Hold Constantinople for 50 turns.
- Control 8 regions for 20 consecutive turns.
- Control 50 regions for a specified period.
- Hold designated ports/straits.
- Survive coalition wars without losing the capital.

Long-term victory families: Domination, Economic, Scientific/Technological, Prestige/Diplomatic, Historical Objectives, and open-ended Sandbox.

## Eras
Eras belong to the persistent campaign, never individual TD maps. Each era must contain substantial internal technological progression and a broad faction-specific arsenal. Era transitions are rare, major campaign events.

## Saves
Campaign state must ultimately persist world ownership, regional statistics, diplomacy, economy, research, objectives, armies/commanders, strategic turn, current battle and exact mid-battle tactical state. Autosave plus multiple manual slots are required for the App Store game.
